How things are

Plaid

Start in sandbox and only move to real banking once the whole loop works — linking, transactions, categorising. Each real connection spends one of a small, permanent allowance that removing a bank does not give back.

Press re-point after changing the server's address, so banks connected earlier keep sending updates. It costs nothing; re-linking would cost a connection.

AI

Entirely optional. With it switched off the budget works exactly as it does now, and anything it would have categorised waits in the review queue instead.
